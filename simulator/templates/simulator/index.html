The shop rules

One batch.
One trading day.

Buy ingredients and bottles before opening. One kit makes one potion. NPC customers buy from your prepared batch; at closing, a wholesaler buys the leftovers at a discount.

Each simulated day is a separate one-day challenge. Recipes, brewing time and stock carryover are outside the model. Busy, Normal and Quiet demand probabilities come from the supplied course example, rather than an existing game. Demand stays fixed when price changes, so price experiments measure margins rather than customer price response.

Calculation and scoring

Cash = selling revenue − production cost + closing clearance.
Textbook score = cash − (selling price − production cost) × unmet demand.
